# Research briefings

One line per document: the question it answers.

- `01-what-wins-forecasting.md` — What actually wins probabilistic forecasting (base rates, equal-weight combination, avoiding overconfidence), and which of it transfers to a system scored on ~31 independent one-year outcomes?
- `02-state-space-and-regimes.md` — Which extensions to the plain five-state Gaussian hidden Markov model earn their complexity, and what does the model's high regime persistence say about what kind of regimes it has actually found?
- `03-all-weather-benchmark.md` — What is Bridgewater's All Weather strategy, what of it is actually public, and what would it cost to build an honest risk-parity benchmark from free, point-in-time data?
- `DESIGN-NOTE-quadrants.md` — Should a four-quadrant branch define its quadrants over growth and inflation levels or over surprises, and how does it choose cut points without look-ahead?
