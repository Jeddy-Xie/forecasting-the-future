ADOPT-A4 · Adopted: the two-chain model is now the pipeline's default

Arm A4 merged, so every command — backtest, forecast-now, submit, the notebooks — runs two chains unless a setting says otherwise. Measured as main's own pipeline against the committed single-chain baseline, which was deliberately not recaptured so the comparison keeps a fixed anchor.

  • It reproduces the arm's figures to the digit, because the arm and the adopted default are the same computation.
  • The submission still carries the old shipped configuration and its manifest; re-shipping needs the owner's token and is a separate decision.
  • Three independent judges adjudicated the stress-test dossier: all three said adopt, two classifying the decision FACTUAL and one MECHANICAL.
  • ADR 0010 records what this does not claim: the mechanism is unestablished, and briefing 05's matched-cell evidence points at granularity rather than timescale separation.

A4 · Two chains: growth, and inflation with rates

Two independent hidden Markov chains replace main's single chain. A growth chain drives growth; a levels chain drives inflation and rates. The joint regime is the pair, sixteen in all, chosen as four by four on a burn-in window ending before the first scored date. The joint transition matrix is the Kronecker product and the fit is exact expectation maximisation, because the likelihood separates by chain.

  • The only arm whose family-wise interval clears zero and survives its independent look-ahead review.
  • Two further seeds, fixed before they ran, reproduce it: one-year lower bounds +0.0299 and +0.0296 (diagnostic 0004).
  • Sixteen regimes on 58 free parameters, against main's six on 89; an unrestricted sixteen-state chain would need 399.
  • Validated for adoption on 2026-09-16: two defects found by executing rather than reading, both fixed, and every scored number unchanged across three re-runs.

A2 · All Weather quadrants over levels, four states

The four quadrants imposed as the state space: every fit has four states, each started from the centroid of one quadrant, split at each series' expanding median so no threshold is written down. The burn-in sweep still runs and would have chosen six; it now chooses nothing.

  • Its registered prediction was that one-year skill would FALL with fewer states. It rose.
  • A model has one state space, so the combination rule takes the larger one-year difference, which is A4's.
  • Its deterministic audit tested main's six states rather than this arm's four (debt D15); its reviewer re-ran the audit forced to four states and it passed.

A3 · All Weather quadrants over surprises

The model reads surprises rather than levels: each month's growth and inflation minus a recursive AR(1) forecast fitted only on earlier months, which is how All Weather's primary sources define the quadrants. State count fixed at four.

  • Faithful to the source, and no better than main.
  • Came closer to the harm floor at five years than any other arm without crossing it.

A1 · Sticky prior on the transition matrix

A Dirichlet prior on each row of the transition matrix, pulling toward staying in the current regime, at a strength fixed at registration from published estimates.

  • Main's fit declares some regime switches effectively impossible, with transition probabilities as small as 1e-184; under the prior the smallest is about 0.0014.
  • Persistence fell as predicted, but the one-year interval straddles zero at both levels.

A0 · Control: main, unchanged

A worktree cut from main with no change at all, run through the same harness as every arm.

  • Exact zeros at every horizon. Without this, a harness that quietly reported main's own numbers for every arm would have looked like six successes.

A6 · Fixed 50/50 blend with the historical base rate

Pure post-processing with the model unchanged: every forecast becomes half the model's probability and half the climatology's, at every horizon and indicator, with no fitted weight.

  • The arm's code does what was registered; the leak is in the registered design. Main's climatology is clean only because main's forecasts never read it.
  • Blending it in gives two known main-level approximations a path into the forecast: the recession lag (at most 0.00083 on a blended forecast) and revised outcome values back to 1919 (up to 0.0047).
  • Neither could move a verdict. The pre-registration voids an arm for any such path, not only a large one, and the rule was applied as written.

A5 · Direct per-horizon regime rates

Instead of composing each horizon from one-month rates through the transition matrix, each regime's rate of the h-horizon outcome is estimated directly, from months whose outcome had resolved and been published by the refit date.

  • Its reviewer rebuilt all 11,730 backtest probabilities from the registered formula with independent date arithmetic; every one matched.
  • The same review found the recession-lag leak now recorded as debt D14.

Integrity findings

Leaks and defects found, each with what it actually cost. These are the findings most likely to matter later, because every one of them would have moved or mis-stated a result silently.

The benchmark counted outcomes before they were published

fixed before any arm ran

Main's climatology benchmark counted each outcome h months after its forecast date, before the observation it rested on had been published: 32 to 400 days early.

main's one-year skill +0.2128 to +0.2154 — paired interval [+0.0021, +0.0028]; no verdict moved

measured by the regression harness; ADR 0009

  • Found by the look-ahead audit the first time it ran, not by review. The pre-registration had already decided the slate halts until main is clean; it halted.
  • The leak flattered the benchmark, not the model.

The recession series' publication lag is too short after a trough

open, deferred until the slate closed

The registry assumes a recession month is known 400 days later. NBER announces turning points, not months: every peak since 1990 came within 400 days (128 to 366), every trough after them (474 to 662). All eight announcement dates were checked at nber.org.

8 of 391 scored forecast dates, two recession indicators only — measured in arm A5 at most 0.0025 on a forecast; in A2, dropping the affected dates moves its one-year difference from +0.0381 to +0.0383

derived from the registry and NBER announcement dates; two independent reviews

  • Main and every arm read the same registry, so it cannot favour one side of a paired comparison.
  • The invariance audit cannot see it: it perturbs by the registered lag, and its own record says so.
  • Fix options: a longer fixed lag of at least 662 days, or availability dated by each NBER announcement.

The look-ahead audit chose the state count its own way

open

The audit's from-scratch run picks the number of regimes by calling the burn-in sweep directly, while the backtest decides it one layer up. An arm that fixes the count in that upper layer gets audited with main's count instead of its own.

one arm affected, A2 — its audit fitted six states and never ran its four-state seeding; its reviewer re-ran the audit forced to four states and it passed, all 2,190 rows identical

found by the arm's author, verified in the code, confirmed by the independent reviewer

  • Every other arm's audit fitted the arm's own count: A3 four, A4 sixteen, the rest leave the count alone.
  • Shape of the fix: one function in the backtest layer decides the count, and both the command line and the audit call it.

What this does not show

  • Every idea was designed by someone who had already seen main's results, so confirmed in sample is the ceiling this design can reach. Out-of-sample confirmation comes only from forecasts registered forward, the first resolving 2027-07-01.
  • Pricing that forward test: about 9.5 months to the first data point, which is ten correlated same-date resolutions rather than one independent observation; roughly three years for a directional read; roughly 31 years to match the backtest's 31.5 independent one-year windows.
  • A4's gain is confounded. The structure and the regime count changed together, and its one-year gain landed on the at-horizon questions (+0.0884) rather than the any-time ones (+0.0297), the opposite of its own hypothesis. The ablation that would separate them reads inconclusive: main's gate rejects sixteen unrestricted regimes as too short-lived and too small.
  • A4 assumes growth is uncorrelated with inflation and rates within each joint regime, and that is false in the data. Measured on the fitting panel, the discarded dependence runs at roughly half to two thirds of the dependence the model does keep: three independent computations — the committed derivation (research/briefings/derivations/within_state_cross_block_correlation.py), an independent reviewer's, and a run against the cached four-by-four fits — put the ratio between 0.51 and 0.67. The exact digits move with which fitted model and which day's panel is measured, so the size is the finding and the third decimal is not.
  • Each A4 forecast rests on thinner evidence: about 45 to 48 months of effective sample per regime, against 112 to 122 for main.
  • Ten-year evidence is about 2.2 independent observations. No ten-year number here is informative.
  • The one-year calibration gate cannot be passed reliably at this sample size: a perfectly calibrated forecaster fails it about 91% of the time at n = 31.

Decisions waiting on you

  1. Adopt A4 as main's method. Merging the branch changes the shipped configuration and needs your shipping token. Under the project's own rule, A4's one-year verdict on the honest start is ship the model, where main's is ship the base rate because main fails the calibration gate.
  2. Register A4's forecasts forward. Beside the shipped configuration's, so both are scored as they resolve from 2027-07-01. It is the only out-of-sample test there is, and it is outward-facing.
  3. The Anvil adjudication. The stress-test dossier leaves decision and classification blank by design: your decision in your words, and one of FACTUAL, MECHANICAL, TASTE or SCOPE.
  4. Push to GitHub. Main is ahead of origin by more than thirty commits and no arm branch has ever been pushed. Nothing outward-facing moves without your word.
  5. How to fix the recession lag (D14). A longer fixed lag of at least 662 days, or availability dated by each NBER announcement. Either lands as its own change, measured against main.
  6. The calibration gate at n = 31. Keep it and report the one-year failure as uninformative, or register a new sample-size-aware test. Moving the threshold is not an option.
  7. Re-shipping. The committed submission still carries the benchmark's pre-fix base rates, which moved by at most 0.0061.
  8. A materiality floor for judged leaks. A6 is void for a leak that moves its one-year difference by 0.0002, because the rule voids for any path. Whether a future registration should state a size below which a found leak is recorded but does not void an arm is a choice for next time.

Candidates next

  • Couple the two chains. The top-ranked extension in briefing 04, and the only one that makes the model more than two separate chains. Diagonal coupling costs four parameters, the forward pass already pays the computational cost, and the measured block-independence violation above is the evidence for it. It carries a free falsifier: the joint second eigenvalue equals the slower chain's only while the chains are independent.
    Provable here? UNDERPOWERED, and aimed at the wrong quantity. Briefing 05 derives the detectable one-year difference as 0.018 at 90% and 0.026 at 98.33%, against a plausible effect near 0.005. It also finds a quantity mismatch: the dependence A4 discards is contemporaneous, in the emission covariance, while coupling changes transitions and leaves that covariance block-diagonal.
  • Time-varying transitions on the growth chain. The factorial structure cuts the parameter cost about twentyfold against putting them on a joint chain, which converts an objection the earlier briefing treated as fatal into a survivable one.
    Provable here? UNDERPOWERED. The parameter-cost claim is proven (12q against 240q, a twentyfold saving), but the forecasting claim needs an effect near 0.005 against a floor of 0.018 to 0.030 — and the proposed driver's own measured skill in this window is negative.
  • A state-count ablation main's gate will accept. The K = 16 ablation could not be fitted. Separating structure from granularity needs a new pre-registration at a count the regimes-exist gate admits.
    Provable here? NOT FALSIFIABLE AS STATED, and provable once rewritten: two chains at 2x3, six joint cells matching main's six exactly, holding granularity fixed and varying only structure. Its two hypotheses predict outcomes about 0.06 apart against a floor near 0.025, and it resolves the confound ADR 0010 admits. Register this one first.
