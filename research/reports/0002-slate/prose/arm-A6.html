What changed. Pure post-processing, with the model unchanged. Every forecast becomes half the model's probability and half the climatology's, at every horizon and for every indicator. There is no fitted weight, because the research showed that fitting one on about 31 outcomes does harm.

What happened. Its numbers alone give no verdict at one year, and it is not harmful. Pulling halfway to climatology gives back some of the model's one-year skill. It helps most at ten years, where main's skill is negative, as the registration predicted, but ten-year evidence is uninformative. The blend was the combination competitions' most robust result, and here it does not beat the model at the horizon that matters.

The prediction that failed. One-year calibration error was registered to fall. By the only measure the tooling produces, the arm's report finds it rose slightly.

Review: leak found, so the arm is void under the rule. The arm's code does what the registration says; the leak is in the registered design. Main's climatology is clean only because main's forecasts never read it. Blending it into the forecast gives two known main-level approximations a way into the forecast:

  • The recession lag (D14). Post-trough labels read before NBER's announcements move the blended forecast by at most 0.00083, on exactly D14's eight dates.
  • Revised values (D5). The climatology averages outcomes from today's revised data, back to 1919, and main's forecasts have no such channel. Rebuilt from archival vintages, industrial production moves the blend by up to 0.0047, and the one-year difference moves by 0.0002.

Neither could move a verdict. The pre-registration voids an arm for any such path, not only a large one, and the rule is applied as written.

Everything else checked clean:

  • There is one publication boundary, not two.
  • The carry-forward never looks ahead and never reaches a scored row.
  • The model parameters are identical to main's.

The arm's own report is wrong on two points:

  • It says no test assertion was loosened, but one was: bool became bool | float. The change was needed for the new setting and hides nothing.
  • It calls the audit an end-to-end check of the blend. The audit sees neither leak channel.
