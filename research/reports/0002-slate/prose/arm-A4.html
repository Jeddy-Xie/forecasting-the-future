What changed. Two independent hidden Markov chains replace main's one: a growth chain, and an inflation-and-rates chain.

  • The joint regime is the pair: sixteen joint regimes, chosen as four by four on the burn-in panel that ends on 1994-01-01.
  • Filtering is main's own forward pass.
  • The fit is exact expectation maximisation, because the likelihood separates by chain.

What happened. It has the largest one-year difference of the six arms, and its one-year 98.33% interval is above zero, and it also improves at five and ten years. Under experiment 0001's rule, its one-year verdict flips from the base rate to the model. At every refit, the joint persistence equals the slower chain's, as predicted.

Two further seeds, fixed before they ran, reproduce it. That rules out a lucky starting point as the explanation (see Is A4 just a lucky seed? below).

Review: clean.

  • Both state counts were chosen before the first forecast date.
  • Forecasts use filtered probabilities. The arm's test fails, by 0.396, if smoothed ones are swapped in.
  • The arm's regimes-exist gate used a restricted null. Against main's full-covariance null on the same panel, the reviewer measured the gap at 1.81 per month in held-out log likelihood. The easier null accounts for 0.08 of it.

Caveats that travel with it.

  • It changes the structure and the state count at once: 16 joint states against 6, with fewer free parameters (58 against 89). So the gain cannot be attributed to the timescale split alone.
  • Each forecast rests on thinner evidence: about 45 to 48 months of effective sample, against 112 to 122 for main.
