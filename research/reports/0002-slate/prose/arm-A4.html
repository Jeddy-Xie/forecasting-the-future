What changed. Two independent hidden Markov chains replace main's one: a growth chain, and an inflation-and-rates chain.

  • The joint regime is the pair: sixteen joint regimes, chosen as four by four on the burn-in panel that ends on 1994-01-01.
  • Filtering is main's own forward pass.
  • The fit is exact expectation maximisation, because the likelihood separates by chain.

What happened. It has the largest one-year difference of the six arms, and its one-year 98.33% interval is above zero, and it also improves at five and ten years. Under experiment 0001's rule, its one-year verdict flips from the base rate to the model. At every refit the joint persistence equals the slower chain's. The arm reported that as a confirmed prediction; it is not one. Briefing 04 showed it is an algebraic identity of the Kronecker product, verified to 2.1e-15, so it checks the implementation and says nothing about whether two timescales help.

Two further seeds, fixed before they ran, reproduce it. That rules out a lucky starting point as the explanation (see Is A4 just a lucky seed? below).

Review: clean.

  • Both state counts were chosen before the first forecast date.
  • Forecasts use filtered probabilities. The arm's test fails, by 0.396, if smoothed ones are swapped in.
  • The arm's regimes-exist gate used a restricted null. Against main's full-covariance null on the same panel, the reviewer measured the gap at 1.81 per month in held-out log likelihood. The easier null accounts for 0.08 of it.

Caveats that travel with it.

  • It changes the structure and the state count at once: 16 joint states against 6, with fewer free parameters (58 against 89). So the gain cannot be attributed to the timescale split alone.
  • Each forecast rests on thinner evidence: about 45 to 48 months of effective sample, against 112 to 122 for main.
  • Two changes arrived together, and the slate cannot separate them: the two-chain structure, and base rates cut on sixteen cells instead of six. The one-year gain landed on the at-horizon questions (+0.0884) rather than the any-time ones (+0.0297), which is the opposite of the arm's own hypothesis.
  • The ablation that would separate them, main refitted at sixteen states, reads inconclusive: main's own gate rejects sixteen unrestricted regimes on this sample as too short-lived and too small (shortest visit 5.6 months, smallest population 1.7%). That is the factorisation's practical argument — sixteen regimes are reachable on 58 parameters and not on 399 — but it leaves the mechanism open.
  • The model assumes growth is uncorrelated with inflation and rates within each joint regime. Measured on the fitting panel, the occupancy-weighted mean absolute correlation is 0.191 with inflation and 0.144 with rates, against 0.288 for the inflation-and-rates pair the model does capture. The discarded dependence is about half the size of the kept one.
